# Unit 02 Topic Map: Chordates

## Syllabus Coverage

- Origin and evolution of Chordates
- Classification, characteristics and adaptive features in Chordates
- Comparative study of organ systems in amniotes and anamniotes
  - Integumentary system
  - Skeletal system
  - Digestive system
  - Respiratory system
  - Circulatory system
  - Urinogenital system
  - Nervous system
  - Sense organs
- Gametogenesis: spermatogenesis and oogenesis
- Fertilization
- Biochemistry of fertilization
- Types of eggs, egg envelopes and cleavage in Chordates
- Foetal membranes with special reference to chick
- Placenta development in mammals
