**Diagram / Flowchart:**

```text
Kingdom Animalia
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

```

- Phylogeny links taxonomy with evolutionary history.

```text

          ↓

          ↓

          ↓
```

**Significance:**

Pseudopodia suit creeping and feeding, cilia suit small aquatic forms, setae help burrowing, jointed limbs support terrestrial success, and tube feet suit slow benthic movement. ### Q5. Explain the canal system in sponges.

**Introduction:**
The canal system is the water-current pathway in Porifera. It supports feeding, respiration, excretion and reproduction because sponges are sessile animals lacking true tissues and organs.

**Diagram / Flowchart:**

```text
Ostia
 ↓
Incurrent canal
 ↓
Choanocyte chamber / spongocoel
 ↓
Osculum
```

**Core Answer:**

**Morphological / Functional Points:**
- Water enters through ostia due to choanocyte flagellar beating.
- Food particles are trapped by choanocyte collars.
- Oxygen diffuses from water into sponge cells.
- Wastes and reproductive cells are carried out through osculum.
- Canal complexity increases from asconoid to syconoid and leuconoid types.

**Significance:**
For structured presentation, classify canal systems clearly. Asconoid is simplest, with water entering directly into spongocoel; syconoid has radial canals; leuconoid has many flagellated chambers and is most efficient. This sequence shows evolutionary improvement in surface area and water circulation. A diagram is essential because Porifera questions are diagram-scoring topics.
