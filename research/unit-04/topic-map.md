# Unit 04 Topic Map: Entomology, Fish and Fisheries

## Coverage Strategy

Unit 4 is divided into **Entomology** and **Fish/Fisheries**. The question bank uses 13 insect-focused answers and 7 fisheries-focused answers to cover morphology, physiology, ecology, applied control, aquaculture and management.

## Entomology Coverage

- Insect taxonomy, body organization, morphology and mouthparts.
- Neuro-endocrinology, insect hormones, metamorphosis and diapause.
- Insect ecology, forensic entomology and medical entomology.
- Bioluminescence, photoreception, communication and pheromones.
- Methods of insect control, insecticides, IPM and economic importance.
- Social behaviour in insects through caste, communication and cooperation.

## Fish and Fisheries Coverage

- Fish morphology, taxonomy, habitat and diversity.
- Organ systems: gills, fins, lateral line, swim bladder and osmoregulation.
- Fish breeding, induced breeding, hatchery seed and brood stock.
- Fish diseases, preservation, processing, culture and polyculture.
- Capture fisheries, pond fishes, cold-water/brackish-water management and aquarium maintenance.

## Diagram Library

- Insect body: Head → Thorax → Abdomen.
- Hormonal control: PTTH → Ecdysone → Moulting.
- IPM: Monitoring → ETL → Control → Evaluation.
- Fish anatomy: Gills + Fins + Lateral line + Swim bladder.
- Aquaculture: Pond preparation → Stocking → Feeding → Harvesting.
